2025 · First of Its Kind

MHSA's Soul Step Charity Walk 2025

MHSA's Soul Step Charity Walk 2025 marked a milestone at the University of Toronto Mississauga as the first mental health charity walk ever hosted on campus. This meaningful initiative brought students together to walk in support of mental health awareness while fostering community, reflection, and collective action.

Through the dedication and generosity of participants, the event successfully raised $1,000 in support of the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH), contributing to vital mental health research, care, and advocacy. Soul Step was more than a walk — it was a step forward for mental health at UTM.

2024–2025 · Centre of Student Engagement

Award-Winning Club

In the 2024–2025 academic year, the UTM Mental Health Student Association was proud to be recognized by the Centre of Student Engagement with two prestigious awards:

Wellness Award

Celebrating dedication to promoting mental health and well-being on campus.

Collaboration Award

Honoring strong partnerships with student groups and the wider UTM community.
