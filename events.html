Our Events

From de-stressors to charity initiatives, our events are designed to support you at every step.

The Mental Health Student Association hosts a variety of different events, including de-stressor activities, community-building initiatives, and educational programming.

🌿

De-Stressor Activities

Relax and recharge with hands-on, calming events — from dog therapy to art and movement sessions.

🤝

Community Building

Connect with peers who understand your journey and build lasting friendships in a welcoming space.

📚

Educational Programming

Learn from guest speakers, workshops, and discussions on topics most relevant to student well-being.
