Mentorship
Program

Connecting students through shared experiences, open conversations, and meaningful support.

The goal of the MHSA Mentorship Program is to foster meaningful relationships between upper-year mentors and mentees while supporting the mental health and well-being of students across campus. Through open conversations and shared experiences, we work to destigmatize mental health and create a supportive space for discussion, growth, and overcoming challenges together.

💬

Open Conversations

A safe space to talk openly about mental health without fear of judgment or stigma.

🌱

Shared Experiences

Learn from upper-year students who have navigated the same challenges you're facing.

🦋

Growth & Resilience

Build resilience and overcome challenges together with peer support and guidance.
