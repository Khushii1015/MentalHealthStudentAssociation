Support & Help

Resources

You don't have to navigate this alone. Here are trusted resources to support your mental wellness.

Whether you need immediate crisis support, on-campus counselling, or general wellness tools, these resources are here for you.

Crisis & National

CAMH

Centre for Addiction and Mental Health — Canada's leading mental health and addictions organization.

Visit Resource Crisis Line

988 Suicide Crisis Helpline

Call or text 988 anytime for free, confidential mental health support in Canada.

Call 988 On-Campus

UTM Health & Counselling

Free counselling and mental health services available to all registered UTM students.

Book Appointment On-Campus

My SSP (Student Support Program)

Free, confidential short-term counselling for U of T students, available 24/7 in multiple languages.

Visit Resource Wellness

Togetherall

Anonymous online community for sharing and supporting mental health, free with a U of T email.

Visit Resource Wellness

U of T Wellness Portal

A comprehensive hub for wellness programs, resources, and supports across all U of T campuses.

Explore Off-Campus

Good2Talk

Free, confidential support for post-secondary students in Ontario, available 24/7.

Visit Resource On-Campus

Campus Safety

The University of Toronto Mississauga Campus Safety Special Constable Service is dedicated to creating a safe, secure, and equitable environment for students, staff and visitors.

Visit Resource On-Campus

ECSpeRT

ECSpeRT is a division of St. John Ambulance; A team of student volunteers with advanced Medical First Responder training to provide fast and effective first aid to all students, staff, and visitors to the University of Toronto at the Mississauga campus.

Visit Resource
